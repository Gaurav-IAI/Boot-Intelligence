Why is mapping required?

Polling-station numbers can change between electoral revisions.

The system therefore does not assume that Part 6 in one year is the same physical booth as Part 6 in another year.

Only evidence-backed relationships are marked Verified.

View methodology

Each row is one record of CEO {{ current_state.state_name }}'s official village-level mapping: a village of a 2003 electoral-roll part and the 2025 roll part (with its building name) that the village moved to. The 2026 polling-station list renumbered stations, so the 2025 part number alone proves nothing about the current part.

A row is Verified only when both official signals point to the same current polling station: the village is listed in that station's areas in the Polling Station List 2026, and the official 2025 building name matches that station's building.

A row stays Review Required when a signal is missing, conflicting or points to more than one station. Where the scanned Polling Station List 2024 (read by OCR) indicates a likely current part, it is shown as “Possible: Part N” only — OCR text similarity is never accepted as verification.

A row is Not mapped when the official mapping lists no 2025 part for the village, or no mapping exists for a historical part.
